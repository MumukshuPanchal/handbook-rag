## Line c, settled

Line c of the note reads: the hardening is not merged as it stands if "the checks of the
reply decline, or take words out of, 4 or more replies to questions the handbook answers
whose own words, as the model wrote them, give the reference answer's fact".

- **Which replies count.** A reply counts when the person lost the answer: the reply as
  shown does not give the fact that answers the question, and the model's own words, kept in
  the saved run, did. A reply from which words were taken out and which still gives the fact
  does not count for line c; such replies are counted and listed apart.
- **Replies read as the fixed form count too.** A reply that the product read as the fixed
  form for a missing part, and showed as the declining sentence, counts for line c on the
  same condition: the model's own words gave the fact. So the reader of line c is shown
  every reply to a question the handbook answers that was changed by a check or read as the
  fixed form.
- **Who reads, and by what rule.** A model session that is neither the maker of the
  hardening nor the session that coordinates the work. For each such reply it is given the
  question, the reference answer, the model's own words and the reply as shown, and not the
  totals of the run. The model's words give the fact if they state what the question asks
  for as the reference answer states it; where a reference answer holds several facts, the
  one that answers the question's main clause is the one that counts; in doubt the reply
  counts, which is the reading less kind to the hardening.
- The number stays 4.

## The limit questions, one line more

The sign test of the note is two-sided. A sixth line, f: the hardening is not merged as it
stands if, of the 40 limit questions, version 2 gives fewer answers with no statement
faulted than version 1 and the sign test over the questions that differ gives p below 0.05.
An instruction to keep limits that makes the answers clearly worse on the questions chosen
for limits would have to be taken out or rewritten first.
